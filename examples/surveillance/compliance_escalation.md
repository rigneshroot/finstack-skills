# Regulatory Compliance Escalation Memorandum

- **To:** Chief Compliance Officer & General Counsel
- **From:** Lead Regulatory Controls Reviewer
- **Date:** October 26, 2025
- **Subject:** **CRITICAL BREACH: SEC Rule 15c3-5 Pre-Trade Risk Control Bypass**
- **Strategy ID:** `EQ_MR_RSI20_US_LC` (Equity Mean Reversion)
- **Breach Status:** **ACTIVE INVESTIGATION - ALL ROUTING SUSPENDED**

---

## 1. Description of Breach
On October 25, 2025 at **14:15:32.412 EST**, our real-time regulatory compliance surveillance systems detected a **Critical Breach** of our pre-trade market access controls on the Equity Mean Reversion execution gateway. 

The trading algorithm successfully routed and executed a single order for **1,500,000 shares** of stock symbol **MSFT** (Microsoft Corp.) at a price of **$324.50**, representing a total single order trade value of **`$486,750,000`** ($486.75 Million). 

This order directly bypassed the pre-trade **Single Order Limit (SOL)** collar, which is hard-coded at a maximum value ceiling of **`$50,000,000`** ($50 Million) per order.

---

## 2. Root Cause Analysis (Gateway Bypass)
The investigation revealed that the model developer (Dr. Alan Vance) modified the execution routing configuration in production at **14:10:05 EST** to achieve "lower execution latency" during a volatility spike. 

The developer redirected the order flow around our **SEC Rule 15c3-5 compliant risk gateway** (which contains the pre-trade SOL and price collars) and routed the orders directly to the broker's raw FIX connection. 

This bypassed all pre-trade credit, capital, and collar validations, exposing the firm to severe regulatory fines and catastrophic tail capital risk:

> [!CAUTION]
> **SEC Rule 15c3-5 Violation:**
> SEC Rule 15c3-5 requires brokers and dealers with market access to maintain *exclusive regulatory control* over all pre-trade risk checks. Allowing a trading desk or developer to bypass these controls, even momentarily, is a direct, severe violation of federal market-access laws.

---

## 3. Immediate Remediation & Enforcement Covenants
To protect the firm from further regulatory exposure and capital risks, the compliance desk has enforced the following immediate actions:
1.  **Trading Suspension:** All capital allocations for strategy `EQ_MR_RSI20_US_LC` are suspended immediately. All open positions are being liquidating in an orderly manner.
2.  **Infrastructure Lock:** The developer's direct production access credentials have been revoked. No further infrastructure modifications can be made without explicit three-signature validation (Quant Research Director, Model Risk Officer, Lead Production Readiness Reviewer).
3.  **Gateway Reinforcement:** The OMS software has been hardcoded to immediately shut down the API socket connection if any outbound order does not contain the cryptographic validation signature generated by the pre-trade risk gateway.
